# Northwind Cloud Systems: Travel and Expense Policy (v4.2)

## 1. Flights
- Economy class for all flights under 6 hours.
- Premium economy is allowed for flights of 6 hours or more.
- Business class needs written approval from a Vice President, and only for flights over 10 hours.
- First class is never reimbursed.

## 2. Hotels
- Up to $250 per night in tier-1 cities (New York, London, San Francisco, Singapore).
- Up to $180 per night everywhere else.

## 3. Meals
- Up to $75 per person per day while travelling.
- Team dinners: up to $60 per person, approved in advance by the team's manager.
- Alcohol: up to 2 drinks per person at approved client or team dinners. Never on solo travel.

## 4. Ground transport
- Taxis and ride-share are allowed. Use economy options (no luxury cars).
- Rental cars only when cheaper than taxis for the full trip.

## 5. Not reimbursable
- Personal entertainment (movies, spa, minibar).
- Upgrades bought with company money.
- Traffic or parking fines.
- Gifts to government officials of any value.

## 6. Receipts and deadlines
- Every expense over $25 needs an itemized receipt.
- Submit expenses within 30 days of the trip.
